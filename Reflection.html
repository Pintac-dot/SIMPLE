Personal Reflection

Let Your Light Shine

Faith teaches us to choose what is right even when nobody is watching. In our daily lives, we should practice honesty, kindness, respect, and responsibility to our friends and fellow students.

Technology is an important part of our lives. We can use it to learn, communicate, and help other people. Because of this, we should use technology responsibly and avoid using it to hurt or deceive others.

The message of "Una ang Ginoo" reminds me to put God first in all things I do. Being a light to others means sharing kindness, helping students and people, and making positive choices both online and offline.
